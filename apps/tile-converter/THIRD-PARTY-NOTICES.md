# @loaders.gl/tile-converter: sources and credits

Esri funded and contributed the original tile-converter development effort.
vis.gl contributors develop and maintain the conversion orchestration, I3S and
3D Tiles adapters, archives and command-line integration in [src](./src).
Original contributions use the [MIT license](./LICENSE).

The application builds on published loaders.gl modules, including the separately
attributed Cesium, Potree, Plasio, Draco, Basis, terrain and archive foundations.
See the [project source inventory](https://github.com/visgl/loaders.gl/blob/master/docs/references-and-credits.md)
and retain the notices from each dependency when redistributing application bundles.

math.gl supplies spatial mathematics; Ajv supplies schema validation; Express,
cors, debug and morgan supply server infrastructure; Inquirer supplies command-line
prompts. Separately installed SDKs and image providers retain their own authorship
and terms. This is a direct contribution inventory, not a transitive dependency
bill of materials. Service content and converted data have independent terms.

Preserve upstream source/version information and applicable full notices when
updating adapters or redistributing generated bundles.
